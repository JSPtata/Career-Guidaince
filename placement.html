Resume Builder & ATS Guide

Before applying to companies, your resume must pass the Applicant Tracking System (ATS). Avoid complex columns and graphics. Use a simple, single-column text format.

❌ Bad Resume

  • Two-column layout (ATS cannot parse properly)
  • Progress bars for skills (e.g., Python 80%)
  • Headshot photos or colourful backgrounds
  • Vague points: "Worked on frontend"

✅ Good ATS Resume

  • Single column layout (e.g. Jake's Resume)
  • Formula: "Accomplished [X] measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"
  • Keywords matching the job description
  • Clickable GitHub, LinkedIn, and Portfolio links

6-Month Preparation Timeline

Assuming your placements start in August (7th Semester), here is how you should structure your final 6 months.

1

Month 1: Resume & Fundamentals

Lock your resume format (1-page standard ATS format). Revise core CS fundamentals: OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, and OOPS. If you are in Core, revise your main subjects.

2

Month 2-4: Intensive DSA / Subject Revision

Solve 2-3 LeetCode problems daily. Master arrays, strings, standard dynamic programming, and graphs. Focus on speed and edge cases.

3

Month 5: Projects & Aptitude

Polish your 2 best projects. Know them inside out—be ready to explain architectural choices. Start taking daily aptitude tests (30 mins daily).

4

Month 6: Mock Interviews

Give mock interviews with seniors or on platforms like Pramp. Practice speaking your thought process out loud while coding.
